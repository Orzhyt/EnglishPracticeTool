# Listening Practice - Unit 1

---

## Question 1

[M] Hello, could you tell me where the nearest post office is?
[F] Sure! Go straight for two blocks and turn right. You'll see it on your left.
[M] Thank you very much!
[F] You're welcome. Have a nice day!

What is the man looking for?

A) A restaurant
B) The post office
C) A supermarket
D) A pharmacy

Answer: B

---

## Question 2

[F] What time does the train to London leave?
[M] The next one leaves at nine fifteen from platform three.
[F] Is there a later one?
[M] Yes, there's one at eleven thirty, but it's usually more crowded.

What time does the next train leave?

A) 9:50 from platform 5
B) 9:15 from platform 3
C) 11:30 from platform 3
D) 9:15 from platform 1

Answer: B

---

## Question 3

[M] I heard you got a new job. Congratulations!
[F] Thanks! I'm really excited about it. I start next Monday.
[M] What will you be doing?
[F] I'll be working as a marketing manager at a tech company.
[M] That sounds like a great opportunity. Good luck!

What is the woman's new role?

A) Software engineer at a tech company
B) Marketing manager at a tech company
C) Sales director at a bank
D) Project manager at a hospital

Answer: B
