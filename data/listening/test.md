# Listening Practice - Daily Conversation

---

## Question 1

[M] Hello, could you tell me where the nearest post office is?
[F] Sure! Go straight for two blocks and turn right. You'll see it on your left.
[M] Thank you very much!
[F] You're welcome. Have a nice day!

What is the man asking about?

A) A restaurant
B) Directions to the post office
C) Where to buy stamps
D) The location of a bank

Answer: B

## Question 2

[F] What time does the train to London leave?
[M] The next one leaves at nine fifteen from platform three.
[F] Is there a later one?
[M] Yes, there's one at eleven thirty, but it's usually more crowded.

When does the next train leave?

A) 9:30 from platform 5
B) 9:15 from platform 3
C) 11:30 from platform 3
D) 9:15 from platform 1

Answer: B
